How to invest in 4 steps

1

Set your capital

Enter how much you want to invest at the top. The Allocation and Shares columns update instantly.

2

Open your broker

Any broker works. On Zerodha, step 3 fills Kite baskets for you. Elsewhere it is one order ticket per name: Copy tickers or Export CSV gives you the list, and the Shares column is each quantity.

3

Place the orders

Use the Shares column as your quantity for each ticker. Limit orders near rec. price are fine on liquid names.

4

Hold for your cycle

Your picks stay fixed for your subscription. Next month's set is published on the 1st — renew to switch to it.

          Filing season: what this book adds to your return
          
        
        
          Your year, with one book: twelve sessions of about ten minutes on the 1st. ITR-2 instead of ITR-1 — your broker's Tax P&L carries the detail. Advance tax, if this year's tax beyond your salary TDS reaches ₹10,000: up to four payments, and the dashboard works out each one. On a 12-month plan, one renewal.
          
        
            
          
          • Your broker's Tax P&L report already lists every trade this book realised. On Zerodha it is in Console, under Reports → Tax P&L.
            • 
          
          • Gains from these books are short-term (section 196 of the Income-tax Act, 2025; section 111A before April 2026), because nothing is held twelve months. With short-term gains you can no longer file ITR-1, and a salaried filer usually moves to ITR-2.
            • 
          
          • At this book's turnover that is roughly  realised transactions a year: one summary in your return, not one line each.
            • 
          
          • Your salary TDS does not cover these gains. If your tax for the year beyond it comes to ₹10,000 or more, it is due as advance tax — by 15 June, 15 September, 15 December and 15 March.
            • 
        
          
        
          Not tax advice: your slab, your other income and your set-offs are yours. Work out advance tax on these gains →
