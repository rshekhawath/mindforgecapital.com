How this compares with what you already have

Picking stocks yourself, funds, PMS, smallcases and tips groups each suit someone. Here is how they differ from a model portfolio you hold yourself.

In short

  • Who holds the money, how you pay, how gains are taxed and how much of your time it takes differ more between these than their returns do.
  • MindForge is a model portfolio: your demat, your orders, a flat fee, and short-term tax because it rebalances monthly.
  • Each option suits someone; the last row says who.
