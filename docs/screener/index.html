Stock screening — common questions

How do I screen NSE stocks by P/E, ROE or other fundamentals?

Enter a minimum or maximum value for any of the 40+ fundamentals — such as P/E, P/B, ROE, ROCE, debt-to-equity, dividend yield or free-cash-flow yield — and the Stock Scanner instantly returns every NSE-listed company that matches. You can combine as many filters as you like and sort the results by any column.

Is the stock screener free?

Yes. The Stock Scanner is free to use and screens 2,000+ NSE-listed companies. No account or sign-up is required to run a screen.

How many stocks and metrics does the screener cover?

It covers more than 2,000 NSE-listed companies across every sector, with over 40 fundamental metrics per company spanning valuation, profitability, growth, leverage and cash flow. Each stock also opens into a full equity report.

Can I save or share a screen?

Yes. The Share button copies a link that reproduces your exact set of filters and sort order, and the CSV button exports the current results so you can keep or analyse them offline.
