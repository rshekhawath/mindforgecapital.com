Glossary

Model portfolio
A published list of stocks or ETFs with a weight for each. You hold it in your own demat and place the orders yourself.
Rebalance
The monthly re-scoring that produces the next list. Only the names that change need an order.
Benchmark or index
The market measure a book is compared with: the Nifty LargeMidcap 250, the Nifty Smallcap 250 or the Nifty 50.
Backtest
A simulation of the rules over past years. Useful for what it shows about a method; not a record of real trading.
Live record
The model portfolio’s real movement since each published rebalance, from 31 August 2026.
CAGR
Compound annual growth rate: the steady yearly return that would turn the starting value into the ending one.
Drawdown
A fall from a previous high, in percent. The deepest one in a period is its maximum drawdown.
Alpha
Return above the benchmark’s over the same period. Here it is quoted only for backtests and says so.
Momentum and trend
The tendency of stocks that have risen relative to others to keep doing so for a while, and its failure in sharp rebounds.
Volatility and liquidity
How much a price swings, and how easily it trades. Both are read from prices and traded volumes.
Free float
The shares available to trade, excluding promoter and locked-in holdings. NSE weights its indices by it.
Equal weight
Every holding the same size. The stock books are equal-weighted; MultiAsset weights its ETFs by score.
Short-term capital gains (STCG)
Tax on gains from shares held for a year or less: 20% on listed equity. Monthly rebalancing makes every gain short-term.
Kite basket
A batch of up to ten orders that Zerodha opens on its own screen, with quantities filled in, for you to confirm.
After-market order (AMO)
An order placed after the market closes, queued for the next morning’s open.
Demat account
The account your broker keeps your shares in. Every share a MindForge member buys stays in their own.
